## 3. What we're working toward

Tripo-quality character models at zero cost, through one interface over local + free-API providers — and those models feeding real characters (the MPersona avatar is the first downstream use). The bar: a model only counts when it is generated by the pipeline, assembled perfectly like its source, and verified in the build it is for. Hand-tooled substitutes are not a style choice; they are disobeying the standing order.

## 4. Standing rules that apply here

- **Branch → PR → merge, always.** Never push to main directly. Add-only, zero-conflict PRs, no force pushes.
- **Checkpoint-and-save law:** CHECKPOINT.md first, update after every step; the repo is the save; resume, never restart.
- **No-temp-storage law:** nothing in /tmp or any wipeable location, ever.
- **Real-source-only:** build from the real sources, wired up fully — no hand-tooled lookalikes.
- **Paid-tool equivalents:** free top-level industry-standard equivalent first; only if literally nothing free reaches the job, study the paid tool clean-room and build our own version. Never bring the owner a purchase list.
- **Licence-not-a-gate:** licence status never blocks pulling in or using material that helps — licence/source is still logged honestly next to the item.
- **No card on any service without his explicit permission.**
- **Never kill in-progress work.**
